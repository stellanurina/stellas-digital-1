About

Hi, I'm Stella.

S

I build websites and WhatsApp AI assistants for small businesses in Indonesia, and I've been making things for the web since 2012.

I started at GroupM (WPP) in Jakarta, producing websites and online campaigns for brands like Unilever and HSBC. My job there was to be the link between clients and developers: turning what a brand needed into something a technical team could build. Since then I've built the digital side of a new Singapore–Japan creative agency.

Being that link is still the heart of what I do. Most business owners don't want to learn about hosting, plugins or APIs. They want more customers, and fewer nights answering the same WhatsApp questions. I turn that into a working website and assistant, and I explain every step in plain language.

I started stellas.digital to bring the standards I learned on global brands to local businesses, at prices that make sense for them. Today's AI tools make that possible: a small business can now have what used to take a whole agency team, from a professional site to content that ranks on Google and an assistant that answers customers around the clock.

When you work with me, you deal with me directly, from the first call to launch and beyond. No account managers and no handoffs.
